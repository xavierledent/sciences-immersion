How does this page work?

In short: switch modes to choose which language shows first, or mix both randomly. Press Listen on each card and repeat the word out loud to train your pronunciation. On each card, tap "I know it" or "Not yet" — or swipe the card: right if you know the word, left if not. Tick "Only words to review" to hide the words you already know; untick it and they all come back. You can change your mind about a word at any time. Keyboard: Space flips the card, K = I know it, N = Not yet, ← → move between cards.

Teacher's tip: try "Français d'abord" mode — say the translation out loud before you flip the card, that's the real test. And little and often works best: a few minutes every day beats one long session the night before the test — come back often!
