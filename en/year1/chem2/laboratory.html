What do we get when we mix substances?

How does this page work?

In short: a real lab, on screen. Each lab starts with a question. Make a hypothesis, then do the experiment yourself and watch carefully what happens. Write your observations in your notebook — the lab does not do it for you. At the end, use your results to answer the question. Underlined words can be translated into French.

Teacher's tip: a scientist trusts results, not first impressions. If your results do not match your hypothesis, that is not a mistake. A wrong hypothesis teaches you something too.
