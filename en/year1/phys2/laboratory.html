How does this page work?

In short: a real lab, on screen, in four steps.

  • Predict: read the question and make a hypothesis.
  • Experiment: do the experiment yourself and watch carefully. Write your observations in your notebook — the lab does not do it for you.
  • Conclude: use your results to answer the question.
  • Check: read your corrected lab report and find your mistakes.
  • Squares: they show how far you have got; tap a coloured square to go back to that step. Each lab changes a little every time, so you can do it again.
  • Underlined words: they can be translated into French.

Teacher's tip: a scientist trusts results, not first impressions. If your results do not match your hypothesis, that is not a mistake. A wrong hypothesis teaches you something too.
