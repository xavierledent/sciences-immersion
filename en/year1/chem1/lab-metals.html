How does a lab work?

  1. 1PredictLook at the samples and make a hypothesis.
  2. 2TestUse the tools and write down what you observe.
  3. 3ConcludeUse your results to answer the question.
  4. 4CheckSee the correction and print your lab report.

In short: a real lab, on screen. Place a sample on the bench, then drag a tool onto it (or tap the tool). Watch carefully what happens, then write your observation in the notebook yourself — the lab does not do it for you. Underlined words can be translated into French.

Teacher's tip: one test is never enough. Something can look right without being right — let the results decide, not your eyes.
