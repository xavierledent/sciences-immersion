Study Tips

  • Challenge yourself: Try the "Français d'abord" mode. Read the French word, say the English translation out loud, and only then flip the card to check if you were right!
  • Train your ear: Click on the loudspeaker icon 🔊 on every card. Listen carefully and repeat the English word 3 times to perfect your pronunciation.
  • Daily routine: Practicing 5 minutes every day is much better than studying for 1 hour the night before the test. Come back often!
